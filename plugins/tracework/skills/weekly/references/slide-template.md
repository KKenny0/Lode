# Weekly Slide Outline Template

Use only when the user explicitly asks for PPT, slides, or an演示大纲. Read
`reporting-narrative-contract.md` first.

The main deck contains 6-10 slides per reporting group. The evidence appendix is
outside the slide budget. Do not allocate slides per work stream.

```markdown
## Slide 1: 本周工作汇报

**日期：** YYYY-MM-DD ~ YYYY-MM-DD
**范围：** {reporting group and projects}

---

## Slide 2: 本周判断

{One repeatable management judgment: starting situation, main movement, end
state, and largest remaining gate.}

---

## Slide 3: 结果弧线一 — {headline}

- **周初约束：** {starting constraint}
- **关键转折：** {decisive movement}
- **周末状态：** {end state}
- **管理意义：** {meaning}
- **剩余门槛：** {gate}
- **证据边界：** {grade}

---

## Slide 4: 结果弧线二 — {headline}

{same structure}

---

## Slide 5: 结果弧线三 — {headline}

{omit when not needed; add a fourth only when genuinely material}

---

## Slide N: 工作组合状态

| 工作主线 | 状态 | 本周形成的变化 | 关系 | 需要关注 |
|----------|------|----------------|------|----------|
| {stream} | {status} | {bounded change} | headline/supporting/portfolio | {gate} |

---

## Slide N+1: 下周收口目标

1. {specific uncertainty or acceptance gate}
2. {target}
3. {target}

### 需要决策或协作

{items or 当前无需要升级的决策或协作事项。}
```

For `all`, create separate group mini-decks with their own judgment and arcs.
Never place work and personal projects in one headline ranking or evidence map.

After the deck, append the same claim-level evidence map defined in
`weekly-brief-template.md`. Keep `O#`, `W#`, `D#`, and `E#` in the appendix or
small footer references rather than the spoken headline.
